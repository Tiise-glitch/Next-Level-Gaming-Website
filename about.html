ABOUT US

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena is a modern entertainment venue based in Johannesburg.

OUR STORY

WHERE GAMING MEETS COMMUNITY

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was established in 2023 by Jason Naidoo, an avid gamer who wanted to create a modern entertainment venue where gamers of all ages could enjoy the latest gaming technology.

The arena provides a space for casual players, competitive gamers, schools, gaming clubs and businesses to enjoy gaming experiences and organised events.
